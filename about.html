What guides the work

Practical improvement over unnecessary complexity.

Every team has a different mix of systems, people and constraints. The best starting point is to understand the work, remove avoidable friction and build solutions people can maintain.

The approach is simple: understand the problem, design with the team, deliver responsibly and keep learning from the results.
