Practical by design

Learn it. Try it. Apply it.

Training works best when people can practise on examples that resemble their day-to-day tasks, with guidance on where AI helps and where it needs checking.

  • Discuss opportunities relevant to your business.
  • Practise prompting and checking AI-generated outputs.
  • Identify data, privacy, security and governance concerns.
  • Leave with realistic next steps and a repeatable way to improve.
